# Bhutanese Culture and Etiquette — Driglam Namzha

> **Language scope:** Bhutanese Dzongkha (རྫོང་ཁ) only — not Standard/Lhasa Tibetan. See `00-language-scope.md`.

Overview of driglam namzha (འགྲིག་ལམ་རྣམ་གཞག) and its connection to Dzongkha language use.

> **Source note:** This summary is aligned with public descriptions from Bhutanese cultural institutions including the Royal Academy of Performing Arts (RAPA) and official Bhutan government cultural materials. Verify details against primary official sources for formal use.

## driglam namzha (code of conduct)
- **Script:** འགྲིག་ལམ་རྣམ་གཞག
- **Romanization:** driglam namzha
- **Meaning:** The traditional Bhutanese code of etiquette, discipline, and graceful conduct.
- **Context:** Governs respectful behavior in daily life, public spaces, and formal settings.

## Language and respect particles
- **Rule:** Adding ལ (la) after greetings and requests softens speech and shows respect.
- **Example:** ཀུ་ཟུ་བཟང་པོ་ལ (kuzuzangpo la) rather than bare ཀུ་ཟུ་བཟང་པོ.
- **Link to driglam namzha:** Polite address reflects cultural emphasis on harmonious social relations.

## Honorific pronouns
- **Rule:** Use ཁྱེད་རང (khyed rang) instead of plain "you" when speaking politely to elders or strangers.
- **Example:** ཁྱེད་རང་ག་འདྲུག (khyed rang ga dru — How are you? — polite)
- **Context:** Honorific language is part of respectful conduct taught under driglam namzha.

## Greetings and formal entry
- **Rule:** Greeting others before beginning conversation is expected in formal and semi-formal settings.
- **Example:** Begin with ཀུ་ཟུ་བཟང་པོ་ལ before asking questions.
- **Context:** Public cultural programs in Bhutan emphasize greeting etiquette as foundational driglam namzha.

## Gratitude expressions
- **Rule:** བཀའ་དྲིན་ཆེན་ལ (kadinchen la — thank you very much) expresses sincere gratitude.
- **Example:** After receiving help: བཀའ་དྲིན་ཆེན་ལ།
- **Context:** Expressing thanks promptly is part of courteous conduct.

## Learning Dzongkha and cultural context
- **Note:** Language learners benefit from pairing vocabulary with etiquette norms rather than memorizing words in isolation.
- **Tip:** When practicing greetings, always include the respectful particle ལ where appropriate.
