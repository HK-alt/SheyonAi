# Core Dzongkha Vocabulary

> **Language scope:** Bhutanese Dzongkha (རྫོང་ཁ) only — not Standard/Lhasa Tibetan. See `00-language-scope.md`.

Essential words for beginner sentences. Each entry includes script, romanization, and an example.

## nga (I / me)
- **Script:** ང
- **Romanization:** nga
- **Part of speech:** pronoun
- **Usage:** First-person singular subject or object.
- **Example:** ང་ལེགས་སོ་རེ། (nga legso re — I am fine.)

## khyed rang (you — polite)
- **Script:** ཁྱེད་རང
- **Romanization:** khyed rang
- **Part of speech:** pronoun
- **Usage:** Polite second-person form.
- **Example:** ཁྱེད་རང་ག་འདྲུག (khyed rang ga dru — How are you?)

## khyim (house / home)
- **Script:** ཁྱིམ
- **Romanization:** khyim
- **Part of speech:** noun
- **Usage:** Refers to a house or home.
- **Example:** ང་གི་ཁྱིམ (nga gi khyim — my house)

## chu (water)
- **Script:** ཆུ
- **Romanization:** chu
- **Part of speech:** noun
- **Usage:** Water in general; common in daily phrases.
- **Example:** ང་ཆུ་ཁོལ་མ་བཏུངས། (nga chu kholma thung — I drink boiled water.)

## gegen (teacher)
- **Script:** དགེ་རྒན
- **Romanization:** gegen
- **Part of speech:** noun
- **Usage:** A teacher or instructor.
- **Example:** ང་དགེ་རྒན་ཡིན། (nga gegen yin — I am a teacher.)

## lobda (student)
- **Script:** སློབ་གྲ
- **Romanization:** lobda
- **Part of speech:** noun
- **Usage:** A student or pupil.
- **Example:** ཁོང་སློབ་གྲ་ཨིང (khong lobda ing — He/She is a student.)

## tsho (food / meal)
- **Script:** ཟ
- **Romanization:** tsho (za)
- **Part of speech:** noun
- **Usage:** Food or a meal.
- **Example:** ང་ཟ་བཟའ། (nga za zay — I eat food.)

## thung (to drink)
- **Script:** བཏུང
- **Romanization:** thung
- **Part of speech:** verb stem
- **Usage:** Verb stem for "drink"; conjugates with auxiliaries.
- **Example:** ང་ཆུ་བཏུངས། (nga chu thung — I drink water.)

## zay (to eat)
- **Script:** བཟའ
- **Romanization:** zay
- **Part of speech:** verb stem
- **Usage:** Verb stem for "eat."
- **Example:** ང་ཟ་བཟའ། (nga za zay — I eat food.)

## gi (possessive particle)
- **Script:** གི
- **Romanization:** gi
- **Part of speech:** particle
- **Usage:** Links possessor to possessed noun (X gi Y = Y of X).
- **Example:** ང་གི་ཁྱིམ (nga gi khyim — my house)
